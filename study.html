Cognitive Contribution Label · Study

What the CCL contributes — and how it compares.

Authorship in the age of AI is no longer binary.

The gap the CCL fills

A rich ecosystem of AI-transparency tools already exists.

Its most original piece is the Human-first label.

Where the law traces “what machine touched the file,” the CCL traces “what mind did the work.”

Comparison with other AI labels

Tool What it documents Reader Verifiable Human focus
CCL The creative process — AI vs human contribution, phase by phase Human No — by design High
C2PA Provenance & edit history of a file Machine Yes (crypto) None
Model Cards / Datasheets How a model or dataset was built Human + machine Partial None
AID / DAISY Which AI tools were used in a manuscript Human No Low
CRediT Contribution roles between co-authors Human No High (people)
CC Signals Permissions for AI training use Human + machine Flexible Partial

The CCL is the only one that combines a focus on the creative process, an explicit account of the irreplaceably human, and a reflective, pedagogical purpose.

Complement, not competitor

vs the EU AI Act

The Act obliges marking the machine's output; the CCL declares the author's process.

vs education guidance

UNESCO and the EU ask for reflection but give no artifact. The CCL is that artifact.

Why non-verifiable is a stance, not a flaw

The CCL is self-declared and cannot be cryptographically proven — and that is deliberate.

Aligned with the fab lab commons

Open, distributed, peer-based, documentation-as-practice.
