A transparent tool to document how much AI contributed to your project — inspired by Creative Commons.

The CCL helps students, researchers, and creators transparently document the role of artificial intelligence across all stages of their creative process. Inspired by the spirit of Creative Commons, the CCL promotes honest reflection, responsible use of AI, and clarity in collaborative, tech-assisted creation.

AI-FIRST

8 phases (Research, Ideation, Design, Coding, Prototyping, Documentation, Management, Reflection). Rate 0–4: how much did AI contribute?

HUMAN-FIRST

6 maker dimensions (Lived Experience, Local Knowledge, Relationship, Body, Risk, Judgment). Rate 0–4: what only you could bring?

BOTH → ARCHETYPE

Combine both to get your authorship archetype: Augmented, Delegated, Craft, or Routine.

WHY IT MATTERS

Not an audit — a reflective practice. The process of honest self-assessment is as valuable as the label itself.
